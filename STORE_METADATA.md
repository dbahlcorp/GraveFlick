## Description

The Last Light Diner is open all night—and tonight, everything outside is hungry.

GraveFlick is an original touch-physics survival comedy built for iPhone and iPad. Grab creatures directly with your finger, fling them into the air, collide them together, and use the pavement to finish the job.

Features:

- Direct grab, flick, throw, and slam controls
- Six enemies with distinct speed, weight, armor, and behavior
- Three recharging weapons and two deployable traps
- Five handcrafted night shifts with escalating waves
- Persistent upgrades, level unlocks, coins, stars, and high scores
- Optional music, sound, haptics, reduced motion, and high contrast
- No advertisements, accounts, analytics, or tracking
