## Promotional text

Grab, launch, and slam the midnight horde across 25 campaign nights, endless survival waves, 15 challenges, and a free-play sandbox at the Last Light Diner.

## Description

The Last Light Diner is open all night—and tonight, everything outside is hungry.

GraveFlick is an original touch-physics survival comedy for iPhone and iPad. Grab creatures directly with your finger, fling them into the air, collide them together, and use the pavement to finish the job before they reach the diner.

MASTER THE NIGHT SHIFT

- Direct grab, flick, throw, and height-sensitive slam controls
- Creature-to-creature collisions, volatile chain reactions, and named combos
- Grave Time, a chargeable special that slows the entire horde
- Nine regular creature types and three distinctive bosses

BUILD YOUR ARSENAL

- Twelve improvised weapons with tap, throw, line, path, and pendulum gestures
- Spike Strip and Flash Freezer traps
- Permanent diner, flick-power, cooldown, and weapon-mastery upgrades

PLAY YOUR WAY

- A 25-night campaign across eight haunted locations
- Endless Survival with roguelite perks and wave modifiers
- 15 standalone challenge missions with special rules
- A free-play sandbox with manual creature, speed, burst, and gravity controls
- Three difficulty levels, stars, coins, high scores, daily rewards, and achievements

PLAY COMFORTABLY

- Independent music, sound-effect, and ambience controls
- Haptics, reduced motion, high contrast, gore, screen-shake, and flash options
- Optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements
- A permanent field guide covering controls, gear, threats, and modes

No advertisements, third-party accounts, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

## Review notes draft

GraveFlick is playable entirely offline. Game Center is disabled by default and can be enabled from Settings; it is not required for gameplay or progression. There are no purchases, ads, analytics SDKs, user accounts, or externally downloaded assets. Cartoon gore can be disabled from Settings.
